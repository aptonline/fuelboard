# Changelog
Follow Fuelboard’s progress with the latest improvements, new features, and fixes. We’re continually refining the experience to make Fuelboard faster, easier to use, and more reliable.
<br>

### `Latest`

## What's New in FuelBoard 1.1.0

**Smarter savings. Clearer comparisons. Better FuelBoard.**

- **Improved Best Value calculations** – Find stations offering genuine savings after factoring in fuel prices and travel costs.
- **Clearer savings information** – See at a glance whether a station saves you money, costs more or breaks even.
- **Refreshed station listings** – Cleaner layouts, improved price ratings and easy-to-read labels.
- **Better controls** – Improved settings, filters and fill-up options.
- **Greater transparency** – Clearer fuel price freshness information and guidance on data accuracy, availability and estimated savings.
- **UI improvements and bug fixes** – More consistent styling, improved accessibility and a smoother experience.

### `Initial Release`
# **Version 1.0**
Welcome to the first release of FuelBoard! Version 1.0 introduces the core FuelBoard experience, giving you a simple and convenient way to stay informed about the price of fuel near you.

This is just the beginning. Future releases will bring further improvements, refinements, and new features based on your feedback.
